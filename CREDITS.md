# Credits

The same credits appear in the app at `#/credits`. Add each new source to both when it first ships.

## Game rules

This work includes material taken from the System Reference Document 5.1 ("SRD 5.1") by Wizards of the Coast LLC and available at https://dnd.wizards.com/resources/systems-reference-document. The SRD 5.1 is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License available at https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.

The rules data was prepared by the [5e-database project](https://github.com/5e-bits/5e-srd-api) by 5e-bits, shared under the MIT License. Only its 2014 SRD 5.1 data is used, imported by `scripts/import-srd.ts` from one pinned commit.

## Dice

The 3D dice are thrown by [dice-box-threejs](https://github.com/3d-dice/dice-box-threejs) from the 3D Dice project by Frank Ali (based on Major Victory's 3D Dice Roller and Teall Dice), running on [three.js](https://threejs.org) and [cannon-es](https://github.com/pmndrs/cannon-es). All three are under the MIT License. None of the library's texture images or sounds are used: the metal and stone finishes are drawn by this project's own code (`src/dice/textures.ts`).

## Battle maps

Battle maps are drawn with [Konva](https://konvajs.org) and react-konva by Anton Lavrenov, under the MIT License. Monster stat blocks come from the SRD 5.1 data above. The map pictures are the DM's own and live only in the private database.

## Pictures

The painted places and moments (`public/art/scenes`), characters (`public/art/people`), ready-made portraits (`public/art/tokens`) and battle maps (`public/maps/*.webp`) that come with the table were made by Matt, the table's DM, with [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai) on 27 October 2026, for this table.

## Voice and cameras

Everyone's voice and camera travel through [VDO.Ninja](https://vdo.ninja) by Steve Seguin, a free and open-source service (AGPL-3.0) shown inside the table in frames. None of its code is copied into this repository.

## Sound

Music, ambience and sound effects play through [Howler.js](https://howlerjs.com) by James Simpson and GoldFire Studios, under the MIT License. The sound files are the DM's own (Envato Elements, licensed for streaming) and live only in the private database, never in this repository.
