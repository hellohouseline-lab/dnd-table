- **C3a: conditions that wear off (live).** The plan is in `docs/C3_PLAN.md`, approved with decisions 72 to 83 as recommended.
  - **How long?** Adding a condition on the Acting now card (Screen 1's Encounter tab and the Battle screen, for monsters and characters alike) asks: **Until I take it off** (as before), **Until the end of their next turn**, 1 to 5 rounds or **1 minute**; a **Save each turn** (the ability and DC, with **Keep the DC secret**); **Ends with** a caster's concentration; and a **Break free DC** for a grab or a web.
  - **Time left** on every chip ("Paralysed · 2 rounds · saves each turn (Wisdom DC 13)"), in the turn order ("Paralysed (2)"), and on a player's own conditions in their turn order. Players never see a secret DC: it's kept in a table only the DM can read.
  - **Counting down** by itself: rounds come off as the creature's turn starts; "until the end of their next turn" ends as it ends; a monster's save each turn is rolled by the server at the end of its turn (the dice only in your roll log; the feed says "Goblin A is still paralysed" or "shakes off being paralysed").
  - **Players** get a card at the end of their turn: **Roll a Wisdom save to shake off Paralysed**. On their turn, **Stand up** (half their speed) and **Break free** (their action, Athletics or Acrobatics, whichever is better).
  - **Concentration:** when the caster's concentration ends (a failed save, a timer, Lost it, or dropping to 0), the conditions tied to their spell end too, with a line in the feed.
  - **One click from spells and attacks:** after a failed save against Hold Person, Command, Entangle, Hideous Laughter, Charm Person, Fear, Blindness/Deafness, Color Spray, Web or Sleep, or a monster attack that causes a condition (read from the free rules: a wolf's bite, a giant frog's grab, a ghoul's claws, a giant spider's web and the like), the feed line offers **Apply Paralysed** (or **Apply Prone (save first)**, which rolls the save for you) with everything filled in. Never applied by itself.
  - **Undead Fortitude** happens by itself: a zombie dropped to 0 by anything but radiant damage or a critical hit makes its save (in your roll log) and may stay up with 1 hit point ("Zombie A refuses to fall!").
  - **When the fight ends,** a minute passes: anything timed to a minute or less, or with a save each turn, comes off. **A long rest** asks whether to clear any conditions left.
  - Undo puts a condition and its clock back.
  - Server: `20261027290000_condition_clocks.sql` (`condition_clocks`, `clock_secrets`, `set_condition`, `condition_save`, `stand_up`, `break_free`, the countdown in the turn functions, Undead Fortitude in the damage step). 2. On Screen 1 press **Start combat**, add two goblins, start. Everyone rolls initiative; the first turn starts by itself.
3. **Opportunity attack:** on a goblin's turn, drag it away from the player's miniature. The player gets "Goblin A is getting away"; press **Attack with ...**. On the player's turn, walk away from a goblin: the chance appears for you along the top of the Battle map.
4. **Spells:** as the player, open **Spells** in the hand. Cast Magic Missile at a goblin (click it on the map or in the turn order). On the next turn cast Darkness: move the mouse over the map and click. Watch the slots go down, "Concentrating: Darkness" appear, and the area show on both screens.
5. **Concentration:** hit the caster with a goblin. A card asks for a Constitution save; roll it. Fail, and Darkness goes.
6. **Death saves:** use the − button on the turn order (Shift-click takes 5) until the player is at 0. On their turn they press **Roll a death save**; the pips show for everyone. Try **Stabilise** and **Up with 1 HP** on the Acting now card.
7. **Areas by hand:** on the Battle screen press **Add an area**, pick a shape, size, name and look, then click the map. Click it again to take it off.
8. Press **U** to undo anything that went wrong.

## Notes for the next session
- Saving a row that may not exist yet: never use an upsert on a table whose campaign_id column can't be updated (stage, dm_notebooks). PostgREST's upsert also sets every column it was sent, including the key, so the update is refused. Update first, then insert if no row came back (`saveStage`, `saveNotebook`).
- Timed effects: `characters.effects` entries may carry `roundsLeft`; `private.tick_effects` (called from `private.fresh_turn` and `end_fight`) counts them down and ends concentration when its timer goes. `supabase/migrations/20261027090000_timed_effects.sql`.
- Bonus attacks go through `public.bonus_attack(attacker, target, roll, kind)` with kind offhand, martialArts, flurry or frenzy (`..100000_bonus_attacks.sql`); the client's `offhandAttack` takes the kind. The catalogue and the "ready" rules are in `src/rules/bonusActions.ts`.
- C1b: the server rules are in `supabase/migrations/20261027020000_damage_types.sql`, `..030000_reactions_death_saves.sql` (with `..031000_one_death_save.sql`) and `..040000_spells_areas.sql`. Attacks and spells share `private.apply_damage` and `private.settle_attack`; `public.cast_spell`, `concentration_save`, `death_save`, `opportunity_attack` and `pass_reaction` are the calls. A trigger on `characters` (`private.on_hit_points`) handles Unconscious, clearing death saves, the concentration save and removing a caster's areas. The same rules for the screens are in `src/rules/damage.ts`, `dying.ts`, `spells.ts` and `areas.ts`, all tested. `src/screens/fight/useCasting.ts` is the casting flow for both screens; `Reactions.tsx` has the strike, death save and concentration cards; `SpellParts.tsx` the slot pips, concentration chip and spell list; `AtAGlance.tsx` the cheat card; `src/live/areas.ts` the areas. The previews mirror it all in `src/screens/dev/previewFight.ts` and `previewAreas.ts`.
