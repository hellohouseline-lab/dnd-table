- **C1b, the rest of the fight's rules (built after the C1 test, live).**
  - **Damage types:** resistance halves, vulnerability doubles, immunity stops it; magic weapons get past resistance to non-magical weapons. Monsters bring theirs from the SRD; players mark their own on the Sheet. The feed says "resisted" or "immune".
  - **Battle screen:** Ask for a roll (A) works mid-fight; a circle shows how far whoever is acting can still move; aiming shows a band for what the attack reaches and a dashed ring where long range gives disadvantage.
  - **Opportunity attacks:** walking out of an enemy's reach without Disengaging gives it a chance to strike. A player gets a card ("Goblin A is getting away": Attack with Glaive, or Let it go); the DM sees every chance along the top of the map and takes a monster's (or a player's, for them). It spends the reaction; the move is never held up; chances close when the turn ends. Glaives, halberds, lances, pikes and whips reach 10 feet, and so do monsters whose attacks say so.
  - **Death saves:** at 0 hit points a character is unconscious, can't move or act, and rolls one death save a turn from Your turn. The pips show to everyone (player, DM's card, party cards). A hit while down is a failure (two for a critical); healing above 0 clears it all; a 20 brings them back with 1 hit point. The DM can roll for them, **Stabilise**, or bring them **Up with 1 HP**. Undo puts it all back.
  - **Spells:** a caster's hand has a Spells set, with slots left as pips above it. A click casts: attack spells roll like weapons; saving throws are rolled on the server for everyone caught (players see a monster pass or fail, never its numbers), with half or no damage on a success; Magic Missile style damage lands; healing heals. The lowest free slot is used, with a picker for a higher one. It spends the action, bonus action or reaction, and the server refuses a second action spell or a missing slot. The DM casts for a player from the Acting now card. Undo gives the slot back.
  - **Concentration:** casting a concentration spell ends the one before. "Concentrating: Darkness" shows with **Lost it**; taking damage brings up a card to roll the Constitution save (DC 10 or half the damage); failing it, or dropping to 0, ends the spell. The DM sees the save owed and can roll it for them.
  - **Areas on the map:** spells that last (Darkness, Silence, Web) leave their shape on the map, placed by the caster: a ghost follows the mouse, and cones and lines turn towards it. Fireball and Burning Hands pick everyone inside and are gone once settled. The DM's **Add an area** draws any shape (a dragon's breath, a fog bank) with a name, a look (harmful, blocks sight, helpful) and Hide from players; clicking an area takes it off. A caster's area goes when their concentration does.
  - **Cheat cards:** At a glance under Your turn (slots, conditions with what they do, features with uses and when they come back), the same on the DM's Acting now card, a C for concentration in the turn order, and slot pips, concentration and death saves on Screen 1's party cards.
  - **Sheet links:** a real link everywhere. From the Battle screen it opens a new browser tab (good for a second monitor); on Screen 1 a click opens the quick panel and Cmd or Ctrl click a new tab.
  - **Left out on purpose (for now):** Magic Missile's darts all go to one target; a character caught in a save spell has the save rolled for them; no rituals; monsters don't cast through spell cards (the DM uses Ask for a roll and Add an area); Undo doesn't bring back an area that went when concentration ended; the "bonus action spell means only a cantrip with your action" rule isn't enforced.

## Notes for the next session
- Saving a row that may not exist yet: never use an upsert on a table whose campaign_id column can't be updated (stage, dm_notebooks). PostgREST's upsert also sets every column it was sent, including the key, so the update is refused. Update first, then insert if no row came back (`saveStage`, `saveNotebook`).
- Timed effects: `characters.effects` entries may carry `roundsLeft`; `private.tick_effects` (called from `private.fresh_turn` and `end_fight`) counts them down and ends concentration when its timer goes. `supabase/migrations/20261027090000_timed_effects.sql`.
- Bonus attacks go through `public.bonus_attack(attacker, target, roll, kind)` with kind offhand, martialArts, flurry or frenzy (`..100000_bonus_attacks.sql`); the client's `offhandAttack` takes the kind. The catalogue and the "ready" rules are in `src/rules/bonusActions.ts`.
- C1b: the server rules are in `supabase/migrations/20261027020000_damage_types.sql`, `..030000_reactions_death_saves.sql` (with `..031000_one_death_save.sql`) and `..040000_spells_areas.sql`.
