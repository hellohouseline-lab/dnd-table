# Paste your notes: using it with Fable (or any AI)

Written for S1d. **Paste your notes** on the Story tab (Screen 1) reads a campaign written in the campaign template and makes your chapters, scenes, people and items from it. The template itself, what each part becomes and the full prompt are in [CAMPAIGN_TEMPLATE.md](CAMPAIGN_TEMPLATE.md).

It all stays private to you: pasted text lives only in your campaign's private database, never in this public code.

## Steps

1. On the Story tab press **Paste your notes**, then **Copy the format for your AI**. That copies the prompt, the template and a short example in one go.
2. Paste it into Fable. At the end, under *My notes:*, write your ideas however they come: the story and tone, how many sessions, the party (for example four level 2 characters), the villains, places and any set pieces you want.
3. The prompt already asks for one chapter per session, fights balanced for your party with a **Balance** line, **Checkpoint** lines for the story beats that matter, **Level up** lines, **Side quest** lines, and an **If someone falls** line for how a character could be brought back. It also asks Fable to list what's missing as questions; answer them and ask it to write the template again.
4. Paste what it writes into **Paste your notes**. The right-hand side shows what it makes, how hard each fight is for your party (worked out from your players' levels), and anything it can't find: a monster not in the free rules, or a picture or map you haven't uploaded yet. Press **Add it to my story**.
5. Tidy anything on the Story tab: pick pictures and music, change a DC, move a scene. Pasting again adds more; people and items with the same name are reused, not doubled.

## Worth knowing

- **Monsters** must be free-rules (SRD 5.1) monsters to make a ready fight. Anything else is kept in the scene's reminders, and the preview tells you which.
- **Pictures, music and maps** are matched by name to what you've uploaded on Screen 2, so upload them first (or pick them on the scene later).
- **Shops** are linked by name, so make the shop first on the Items tab.
- **Loose notes** work too: headings become chapters and scenes, lines starting `> ` become read-aloud, and everything else becomes reminders.
- **Automatic campaign building** (giving the table a whole file and having it chop it up, make shops and stock them) is a later phase.
