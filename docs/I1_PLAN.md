# I1: items, money, loot and shops

Written after C2 and the levelling-up fix. **Approved by Matt with decisions 38 to 54 as recommended.** It builds sections 3 and 4 of `TABLE_TOOLS_PLAN.md` in their simplest form, on top of the wallet and inventory C2 already added, and keeps the test one-shot on 21 to 22 November in view.

## In plain words

When the party finds something, buys something or hands something to a friend, the table moves it for you: the item lands in the right pack with a sound, the coins come out of the right purse with change made, and the log says who got what. You never have to type an item in or do the sums.

Three things arrive, one milestone each:

1. **Giving:** you give items and gold (to one player, or split across the party), and players hand things to each other.
2. **Searching and loot:** a player searches a chest or a body, rolls, and the table suggests what they find from our loot tables. You check it and press **Give**.
3. **Shops:** you open a shop; players browse it on their own screen and buy or sell, and you can undo any sale.

## What's already there (from C2)

- Every character has coins (copper, silver, electrum, gold, platinum) and a pack with weights. Wearing armour sets the AC.
- **Change your character** has every weapon, armour, piece of gear, kit and potion in the free rules, as cards with plain words. Adding there is free.
- Potions, a healer's kit, alchemist's fire and the like can be used in a fight.
- The DM's notebook has **Key items to collect**, with who has each.
- On Screen 1, the **Items and loot** tab and the **Give an item** button are there but greyed out. I1 brings them to life.

## After I1a: Matt's notes

Having tried I1a, Matt asked for more before searching and shops. This adds a milestone, **I1a+**, and changes I1b from "search with a roll" to "open a box".

**Browse, don't search.** The Give window becomes a scrollable grid of cards with toggle tags, so you can skim and tick:

- **Rarity:** Common, Uncommon, Rare, Very rare, Legendary. (The free rules have only 4 common magic items; most low-level treasure is uncommon.)
- **Type:** Weapons, Armour, Potions, Rings, Amulets, Cloaks, Boots and gloves, Belts, Headwear, Bags, Wands, Staffs and rods, Scrolls, Other. The free rules lump 167 items together as "wondrous items", so the table sorts them into these by name.
- **Where from:** Magic items, Everyday gear, Bits and bobs, Your items.
- Tick several cards and give them all to one person at once.

**Your items.** You make your own items once (a name, what it is, a description, tags, and **Key item** if it matters to the story) and give them whenever. They're kept privately in your campaign; players see the name and description only once they're given it. Giving a key item fills in who has it in your notebook.

**Bits and bobs.** Ordinary odds and ends written for this table: a bent spoon, a ball of string, a scrap of cloth, a chipped cup, a candle stub, a wooden comb, a tarnished button. They're in the browser and in boxes.

**Coins at a glance.** On the Items and loot tab each character's purse is drawn as coins (platinum, gold, silver, copper), with quick buttons to add or take **1, 5, 10, 50 or 100 gp** in one click (change made when taking).

**Buying from a player.** **Offer** next to any item on a character's card: you set a price in gold, the player gets the offer on their screen and presses **Sell** or **No thanks**. If they sell, the item leaves their pack and the coins arrive.

**Open a box** (I1b, replacing Search). Pick the container (a chest, a sack, a body, a desk or cupboard, a hoard) and how rich it is (junk, ordinary, valuable, treasure), and press **Open it**. The table rolls a mix of bits and bobs, coins, gear and, at the richer end, magic items. You can re-roll, swap or remove any item and add your own, then **Give** it to one person or **Put it on the table** for the party to take from. An Investigation roll first is optional: open it straight away, or let the roll decide how much they find.

**How rare, by level.** The free rules don't include the DMG's treasure tables, so ours follow the well-known guide to which rarities suit which levels: up to uncommon at levels 1 to 4, rare from 5, very rare from 11, legendary from 17. A box only rolls magic items up to the party's band; richness changes how many. You can always hand-pick anything.

## 1. Giving items and gold (I1a)

**Magic items.** The free rules include about 360 magic items (Bag of Holding, Cloak of Protection, Wand of Magic Missiles, the potions and more). They come in from the same 2014 free-rules data as everything else, with their rarity, whether they need attuning and what they do.

**Give an item** (the verb button, and on each card in the Party tab):

1. Search everything: gear, weapons, armour, magic items, or type in something of your own ("A letter sealed with black wax").
2. Pick who gets it, and how many.
3. **Give.** The item lands in their pack with a sound and a card that flies in on their screen. The log says "Thorin got a Potion of Healing".

**Magic weapons and armour.** A +1, +2 or +3 weapon or armour is picked as the weapon itself plus the bonus ("Longsword +1"). The bonus goes on the attack card and the AC by itself.

**Attunement.** A magic item that needs attuning has an **Attune** button on the sheet. The table allows three at a time, as the rules say.

**Give gold** (the same button, a Coins tab):

- to one player, or **Split across the party**, which shares it evenly and makes change in silver and copper. Anything that can't be split stays in the party stash.
- **Take gold** works the same way, for a fine, a bribe or a toll.

**Players hand things over.** On the Inventory tab, **Give to…** next to any item or the coins, then pick a friend. Outside a fight it's instant. In a fight, potions work as they do now (it takes the action); other things wait until the fight ends.

**The Items and loot tab on Screen 1** shows every character's pack and purse side by side, so you can see who has the rope.

**Key items.** Giving an item that's on your **Key items to collect** list fills in who has it.

## 2. Searching and loot (I1b)

**Search** (on the Items and loot tab, and as a verb button):

1. Pick who searches, the place (a room indoors, outdoors, a dungeon, a body) and how rich it is (junk, ordinary, valuable, treasure).
2. The table asks them for an Investigation check (or Perception, for something hidden). They roll as they do now.
3. The table suggests what they find, from the roll:
   - **under 10:** junk, or nothing;
   - **10 to 14:** one real find;
   - **15 to 19:** a find and some coins;
   - **20 or more:** all that, and something extra.
4. **You see the finds first.** Swap any of them, take one away, add your own, or add a key item you've put there. Then press **Give** (to the searcher) or **Put on the table** (to the party stash).

**Our loot tables.** The free rules don't include the DMG's treasure tables, so these are ours, written for this table: a cracked lantern, 3 copper pieces, a bent spoon, a silver ring worth 10 gp, a Potion of Healing in a treasure room. There's one table for each place and richness. You can edit them on the Items and loot tab; your changes are private to your campaign.

**The party stash.** Loot put on the table goes into a shared stash every player can see. Anyone can **Take** an item, and **Split the coins** shares the money evenly with one click. Anything nobody takes stays in the stash for later.

## 3. Shops (I1c)

**Make a shop** on the Items and loot tab:

- **What kind:** a general store, a blacksmith (weapons and armour), an alchemist (potions, oil, acid), a fletcher (bows and arrows) or a travelling merchant (a few survival things). Each fills its shelves from the free rules at their real prices, with how many of each.
- **A name and a shopkeeper,** with one line about them ("Brenna, who never stops talking about her son in the city guard").
- **Prices:** cheap, fair or pricey (80%, 100% or 125% of the book price). Change any single price or how many there are, take things away, or add magic items with a price you choose.

**Open the shop** and players see a **Shop** button on their table screen. It opens the same cards as Change your character, with prices:

- **Buy** takes the coins from their purse (with change made) and puts the item in their pack. The table refuses if they can't afford it, or if the shop has run out.
- **Sell** offers half the book price for gear, as the 2014 rules suggest. You can set a shop not to buy.

**You see each sale** on Screen 1 as it happens ("Wren bought a Hempen rope for 1 gp"), with **Undo**. **Close the shop** and it disappears from players' screens until you open it again.

**Adding gear for free** on Change your character becomes a table setting once shops exist: on by default, so players can add something you gave them at the table. Turn it off and only you can.

## Hidden means hidden

- A shop's shelves and prices reach players only while it's open.
- Search finds reach nobody until you press **Give** or **Put on the table**.
- Your edited loot tables and your notes on shopkeepers are yours only.
- Every move of an item or a coin happens on the server, which checks it's allowed: a player can only hand over their own things, only buy what they can afford, and only take from the stash.

## How it's built

- **Rules (with unit tests):** coins and change (paying from the smallest coins up and breaking a bigger coin when needed, change back in the fewest coins), splitting coins across the party, selling prices, the attunement limit, magic bonuses on attacks and AC, and turning a search roll into finds.
- **Data:** the free rules' magic items added to the import, loaded only when needed. Our loot tables in the code as the starting set.
- **Server:** new tables for shops, sales, the party stash and edited loot tables, each with row-level security. Server functions for giving, handing over, splitting, buying, selling, undoing a sale and taking from the stash, with database tests for each, including the ones that must be refused.
- **Live updates:** items and coins move through the characters' rows, which every screen already follows. The card flying in and its sound go out as a short broadcast.
- **Free plan:** everything here is small text. No new storage or uploads.

## Decisions for Matt (recommendations first)

38. **Splitting gold:** evenly, with change made in silver and copper; anything that can't be split stays in the party stash. *Recommended.* Or the DM picks who gets the odd coins.
39. **Making change:** pay from the smallest coins up, breaking a bigger coin when needed, with change back in the fewest coins. *Recommended,* so purses stay as players expect. Or turn every purse into gold first.
40. **Selling:** shops buy gear at half the book price, and you can set a shop not to buy. *Recommended.* Or shops don't buy at all.
41. **Magic item prices:** the free rules don't price magic items, so you set each one when you stock a shop, with a Potion of Healing suggested at 50 gp. *Recommended.* Or magic items aren't sold in shops.
42. **Loot tables:** ours, written for this table, which you can edit and keep private to your campaign. *Recommended* (and already agreed in decision 26).
43. **Search results by roll:** under 10 junk or nothing, 10 to 14 one find, 15 to 19 a find and coins, 20 or more something extra, and you always check before Give. *Recommended.* Or no roll: you just pick the finds.
44. **The party stash:** loot put on the table goes into a shared stash any player can take from, with one-click coin splitting. *Recommended.* Or you hand every find to someone yourself.
45. **Handing things over in a fight:** only potions (as now); everything else waits until the fight ends. *Recommended,* to keep fights simple. Or allow any item, using the turn's free object interaction.
46. **Adding gear for free** on Change your character: a table setting, on by default. *Recommended.* Or off once shops exist.
47. **Unidentified items** ("an unknown potion" until someone casts Identify or takes a short rest with it): leave until after the one-shot. *Recommended,* as they need a secret store of their own. Or include them in I1b.
48. **NPCs:** shops get a shopkeeper's name and one line for now; the full NPCs tab (portraits, Show an NPC, a random NPC) comes with S1, where your campaign's people live. *Recommended.* Or build the NPCs tab in I1.
49. **Work that takes time** (repairs, upgrades that are "ready in 3 days"): leave out of I1. *Recommended.* Or add it to I1c.
50. **Locks and traps:** leave to C3 lite; **Ask for a roll** with a DC covers them for now. *Recommended.* Or add them to I1b.

51. **Your items** are private to your campaign, and players see an item's description once they have it. *Recommended.* Or players see only its name.
52. **Buying from a player:** the player accepts or turns down your offer on their screen. *Recommended,* as it's their item. Or you take it and pay at once.
53. **Rarity by party level** (up to uncommon at 1 to 4, rare from 5, very rare from 11, legendary from 17), with richness setting how many. *Recommended.* Or richness alone decides.
54. **Quick coin buttons** in gold only (1, 5, 10, 50, 100 gp), with the purse drawn as coins. *Recommended.* Or a button for each kind of coin.

## What stays out (for now)

- The DMG's treasure, magic item and price tables, which aren't in the free rules. Ours stand in.
- Unidentified items, containers (a Bag of Holding is an item, but things can't go inside it yet), and dragging cards between packs.
- Encumbrance beyond showing the weight carried against what they can carry.
- Crafting, and orders that take days.
- Magic items from Open5e, which can be added later through the same import.
